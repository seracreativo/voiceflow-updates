Before you pay

You don't have to take our word for it.

When you install it you get 14 days with everything in Pro, no account and no card. When they end nothing breaks and nothing is deleted: the extras go and you keep dictating on the free plan.

Refunds

Thirty days, no questions. You get the amount back minus the payment processor's fee, around €2: the bank keeps that when charging and it doesn't come back on a refund.

At checkout you tick a box asking for immediate delivery, and that gives up the fourteen days of withdrawal the law grants you. It's what lets us send the licence the same day instead of waiting two weeks, and these thirty days are what you get in exchange. It's all in the terms of purchase.

If you change computer

The licence travels in your iCloud Keychain, so it shows up on its own. And if you'd rather not depend on that, keep the text you were sent: it activates it again whenever you want.
