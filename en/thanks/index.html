01 · Right now

What's going to happen.

Your licence is issued by hand and goes out by email. It usually takes a few hours, and at most a day.

It isn't for the craft of it: issuing licences automatically means putting the signing key on a server, and that key is the one thing in this project whose theft would force a new licence on everybody. While sales fit into a few minutes a day, it stays where it is.

  • If you pasted your account code when paying, it'll arrive already linked: paste it in and you're done.
  • If you didn't, we'll write to ask for it, or send it over and skip a step.
  • Meanwhile you keep dictating. The 14 days with everything in Pro haven't gone anywhere.

02 · Your code

In case you haven't sent it yet.

It's in the app, under Licence in the sidebar: the Link my account button copies it to the clipboard. It's a short, meaningless string, not your Apple ID.

Reply with that code and the licence goes out in the next batch.

Don't use iCloud? Say so and we'll send an unlinked licence, which works just the same on any Mac.

03 · If something fails

Just write.

If a day goes by and nothing has arrived, reply to the Stripe receipt or write to us directly. Keep that receipt: it carries the order number, and with it your licence can be reissued if you ever lose it.
