VoiceFlow 1.4.1

October 27, 2026

  • VoiceFlow no longer quits when the microphone goes away. A USB webcam dropping off for a moment, or the iPhone's mic coming and going through Continuity, left the app broken inside and it would close on its own seconds or minutes later. Now, if there's no microphone when you start dictating, it tells you and stays open.
  • Dictated text no longer shows up twice in some apps. When an app was slow to show what it had received, VoiceFlow assumed it hadn't arrived and pasted it again.
