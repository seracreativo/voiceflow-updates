VoiceFlow 1.3.2

August 26, 2026

  • Stumbling at the start of a sentence gets cleaned up now. «I I think so» survived untouched because the rule was case-sensitive, and the speech engine capitalises the start of every sentence — which is exactly where people stumble most.
  • «I mean» and «sort of» come out even with a single comma around them. The engine punctuates by intonation and doesn't always put both in.
  • Corrections no longer take a word with them. «Let's meet Monday, no, make it Tuesday» left behind «Tuesday» and swallowed the «Let's meet».
