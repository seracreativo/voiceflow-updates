What it does

Dictating fast isn't the point
if you have to go back over it.

Every dictation app writes down what it hears. The work starts after that: taking out the «uh», putting in the commas, and dropping the half-sentence you corrected mid-thought. VoiceFlow does that before the text reaches the screen, and it does it on your Mac.

01 · The gesture

One key. No windows, no buttons.

Hold it down, talk, let go. The text appears where your cursor was, in whatever app you happen to be in — no plugins and no integrations.

  • Works in any app, including the terminal
  • Nothing to click: no windows and no popups
  • Offline, and just as fast

02 · Polish

Three levels, and a different one per app.

In the terminal you want exactly what you said. In an email you want it to read well. VoiceFlow remembers which level you use in each app and applies it on its own.

  • Verbatim — as spoken, untouched
  • Clean — filler words out, punctuation in
  • Polished — reordered so it reads in one go

03 · Corrections

You correct yourself out loud, it keeps the good one.

Talking isn't writing. You stumble, you say a time and change it halfway through. When you mark the correction, VoiceFlow keeps the good version and drops the other — before anything is typed, not after.

  • «forty euros, sorry, fifty» — fifty gets typed
  • «Monday, no, make it Tuesday» — Tuesday gets typed
  • Two corrections in one dictation, each in its place

04 · Translator

Select, press, and it's translated.

Select text in any app, press the shortcut, and a small panel shows the translation. Copy it, or replace the original in place.

It runs on your Mac too: the language pack downloads once and never again.

05 · Your words

Odd names, spelled right.

Proper nouns, acronyms, jargon, your client's surname. You tell it once and they always come out exactly as you spelled them, however the speech engine hears them.

  • Handed to the speech engine before transcribing, not after
  • Unlimited terms in Pro
  • Synced across your devices

06 · The engine

And if you want more accuracy, it downloads.

By default VoiceFlow uses the speech engine macOS 26 already ships with. That's why the download is 6.5 MB and it dictates from the first minute: nothing to fetch.

Whisper is there too, switched off, for anyone who wants it. It raises accuracy and it raises the weight — and it also runs on your Mac.

Privacy

It's not that we don't sell your data.
It's that we don't have it.

The transcription is done by the system's own speech engine, and the cleanup by rules and the language model macOS already ships with. There is no server of ours in the middle: neither the audio nor the text is sent anywhere, and VoiceFlow works the same with no connection.

That isn't a promise on a web page. It's how it's built: the code that would send anything doesn't exist. The app has no accounts and no telemetry — we don't know how many people use it, what they dictate, or when. This site counts visits, without cookies and without identifying anyone, which is why it never asked you to accept anything.

Before you start

The first time you open it.

macOS will ask for two permissions, and it's worth knowing why: the microphone, so it can hear you, and Accessibility, which is the only way macOS lets an app type inside another one. It doesn't ask for screen recording, and it never will.
