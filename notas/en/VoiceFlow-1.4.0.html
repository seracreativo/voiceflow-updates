VoiceFlow 1.4.0

August 26, 2026

  • VoiceFlow speaks English. If your Mac is in English, so is the whole app: the screens, the error messages, and the system dialogs asking for the microphone and for speech recognition. Release notes too, which until now always came in Spanish.
  • Along the way: the app was telling macOS it was an English app while every one of its texts was in Spanish. That's why the update dialog came up in English on a Mac set to Spanish — the system handed Sparkle the language the app claimed, not the one it had. It now says the one it is.
