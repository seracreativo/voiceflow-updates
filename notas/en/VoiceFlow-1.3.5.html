VoiceFlow 1.3.5

August 26, 2026

  • Your own terms now reach Whisper. If you had it switched on, the vocabulary you'd taught it wasn't being handed over — «AppDelegate» came out as «app de Legate» — even though it worked fine with the Apple engine. They're given to it before transcribing now, the way it was promised.
  • Translation is no longer polished by the on-device model. What it actually did got measured: in 8 out of 10 sentences it handed back the translation untouched, and of the 2 it did rewrite, it made both worse — «no me da la vida esta semana» came out as «I am not feeling well this week», which means something else. Without a cloud model the translation comes out faithful: correct, and half a second faster. Settings says so now instead of leaving a switch on that did nothing.
  • Settings you change on another device show up straight away. They arrived over iCloud and were saved correctly, but the screen kept showing the old value until you restarted the app — and touching that same setting wrote over it and undid what had arrived.
